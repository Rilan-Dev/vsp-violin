03

The gaps that are not about content

Blocks launch

Mobile and tablet do not exist yet

Everything built so far is desktop-width. Your primary audience — diaspora parents — will mostly arrive on a phone, and the four-column menu, the hero split and the three-card enrol row all need composing again at narrow widths, not shrinking. This is the single largest remaining piece of design work.

Blocks launch

38 old addresses need forwarding

Your lesson links are in YouTube descriptions, WhatsApp threads and Google's index. Each of the 38 old URLs must redirect to its new home or that audience lands on nothing.

Studio, 3 of 4

The publish flow is one screen of four

"New notation" is designed. Still needed: the enquiries inbox so no message goes unread, the lesson list for editing and reordering what is already published, and the category manager for adding a menu like Thillana. Plus the login screen itself.

Assets

Three photos in, and a logo still missing

Your new session gave three usable portraits, all now placed. Still needed: the logo as a vector file (only low-resolution PNGs exist), photographs of the twelve awards, and the performance photography for the Stage page. The blue kurta reads well against the violet, so more from that session would sit naturally.

Six lessons still have no title

The 2023 batch shipped untitled on the live site. I have inferred names from the notation filenames — Mohanam Varnam, Enna Thavam, Maha Ganapathim, Lingashtagam, Poongathave, Ilayaraja Hits — and need you to confirm or correct them. Two lessons also carry the wrong heading copied from a neighbour.
