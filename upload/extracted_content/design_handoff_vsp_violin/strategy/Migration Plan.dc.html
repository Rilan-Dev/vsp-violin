Two sites into one, and who can touch what.

Where the build stands, what is left, and how the roles actually work on your two existing sites — including one correction to the assumption in your message.

01

Your understanding of the roles — mostly right

Correct

One owner, no other logins

Neither site has student accounts, registration, or a members area. Visitors read and download; only you publish. That is exactly the model to carry forward.

Correct

Visitor and owner journeys are separate

Two different workflows, no overlap. Nothing a visitor does can change the site, and publishing never happens on a public page.

Needs correcting

There is no admin panel today

sukapavalan.com is a Blogger site — your "admin" is Google's Blogger dashboard, not something built for you. vspviolinrainbow.com has no admin at all; it is fixed pages, which is why its lesson lists sit on "Loading…" forever. So the studio panel is new work, not a migration.

Three more things worth knowing before we lock the model
01

Visitors can already post publicly. Blogger comments are open — there are live comments on your profile and lesson pages, including one from July asking whether you take classes. In the new site I would keep comments off and route that person to the enquiry form instead, so no reply is ever missed.

02

Your notation is not really protected. Every PDF is a public Google Drive or Dropbox share link — anyone can forward it. That is fine if free is the strategy, and it is your best marketing. But it means "free library" is a decision, not an accident, and if you ever want a paid tier the files must move to storage the site controls.

03

One login is a single point of failure. If you lose that account, the site cannot be updated. I would set up a second owner login for a family member from day one — not a public role, just a spare key.

02

The two workflows, side by side

Same content, two doors. The visitor door is public and needs no account. The owner door sits behind a login on a separate address and is never linked from the public site.

Visitor

Public · no login
01Arrives from YouTube, Google or word of mouth
02Opens Lessons → picks a category → opens a lesson
03Reads notation in English or Tamil, plays practice audio at their sruthi
04Practises for weeks. Returns. Downloads more.
05Hits a limit alone → asks for a trial lesson. The only thing they can submit.

Cannot: log in, comment, upload, edit, or see anything unpublished.

You, the owner

Private · login required
01Signs in at a separate address, not linked publicly
02Reads new enquiries — trials, bookings, collaborations
03New notation: title, category, raga, thala, PDFs, video links
04Saves a draft, previews it, then publishes when ready
05Menu, library grid, raga index and sitemap update themselves

Also: add or rename a category, reorder lessons, replace photos, edit any page text.

05

Four decisions I need from you

I can keep designing pages without these. I cannot finish without them.

1

Self-publishing, or send them to me?

The studio panel means a login, a database and file storage — a real second phase with a small monthly cost, and full independence for you. The alternative is that categories live in one file and you send me each new lesson. Slower for you, nothing to run.

2

Do we publish fees?

Right now the site says "free trial" and nothing else. A published fee filters out the people who were never going to pay and makes the serious ones enquire with intent. Your call — per class, per month, or per term.

3

Which domain wins?

My recommendation is sukapavalan.com — it is your name, it carries the search history, and it is easier to say aloud. vspviolinrainbow.com then forwards to it. If the client prefers otherwise, the redirects simply run the other way.
