01

Where the business actually stands

Problem 01

Two businesses, two websites, neither complete

vspviolinrainbow.com is the brochure — beautiful claims, zero product. sukapavalan.com holds the actual product: 22 notation lessons, Tamil and English PDFs, audio in five sruthis, video progressions. A visitor who lands on the brochure never finds the thing worth paying for.

Problem 02

The credibility is buried

Twelve honorific titles, All India Radio since 1992, Thyagaraja Aradhana every year since 1992, six television channels, US tours across six years, gurus of real standing. Today this reads as a list. It should be the first thing a parent in New Jersey feels.

Problem 03

Nothing asks for the sale

There is no fee, no schedule, no trial, no enrolment. A visitor who wants to learn has one option: a contact form. Meanwhile visitors are literally commenting "Are you taking violin classes??? Kindly let me have the information."

Problem 04

Visible signs of neglect

Loreip placeholder text sits inside two live page headlines. Lesson lists never load. Six posts ship with no title. Six nav links point at htttps://. Notation is split across Google Drive and Dropbox.

The asset is not the website. The asset is a 37-year teaching practice with a bilingual notation system nobody else has. The rebuild's only job is to make that legible, then make it purchasable.

02

Who this is for, ranked by attractiveness

Four real audiences exist in the evidence — his own testimonials, his comment threads, his performance record. They are not equally worth designing for. Ratings below are mine; the figures behind them are sourced at the end.

A Primary

Diaspora parents buying their child a root system

Tamil and wider South Indian families in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, Singapore and the Gulf, with children aged 6 to 16. They are not shopping for a hobby class. They are trying to hand down something they are afraid of losing, and they will pay for a guru with a lineage rather than a platform with a catalogue. Two of his four testimonials are exactly this family, writing from Los Angeles and St. Augustine.

Where sources disagree

Mordor puts the 2026 market at $4.61B. Research and Markets says $2.18B in 2025 rising to $4.9B by 2030. Global Growth Insights and Business Research Insights both claim $23.5B in 2026 — five times higher. Treat the low-single-digit-billions figures as the defensible ones and the $20B+ figures as including adjacent categories. None of this should drive a pricing decision on its own.

What competitors charge

Shankar Mahadevan Academy lists USD 329 for a Carnatic vocal course and reports 11,000+ students in 47 countries. Swaralaya starts at AUD 60/month for four one-to-one classes with 60+ teachers. BMusician undercuts private tuition by 40–50% with shared slots. Independent online violin instruction runs $40–70/hr, and $80–120/hr for experienced teachers.

04

The gap we are designing into

He cannot win on catalogue — the academies have hundreds of courses and dozens of teachers. He cannot win on price — shared-slot platforms are half the cost. He wins on the three things a platform structurally cannot offer.

Advantage 01

One named guru, not an allocated teacher

A platform assigns you someone. Here you learn from the man whose gurus were Thiruvarur Santhanam, Nellai Shanmuganathan, Mayavaram Sivaswamy Iyer, V. L. Sudharsan and Kumaresh. Lineage is the product. The site must name it on the first screen.

Advantage 02

Notation in the language the family speaks

Every one of the 22 lessons already exists as a Tamil PDF and an English PDF. No competitor leads with this. For a child who reads English and a grandparent who reads Tamil, it is the whole decision.

Advantage 03

A free library that already converts

Practice tracks in five sruthis across three speeds. Violin and vocal recorded separately. Step-by-step, then practise-together, then karaoke. Given away. That generosity is the marketing, and it is currently hidden on a Blogger template.

The visual thesis · one sentence

A darkened concert hall where the notation is the only light — brass for what he has earned, ivory for what he teaches, and nothing on the screen that a musician would not recognise.

05

Art direction, locked

Palette — five colours, defined roles

Stage Ink · #0A0A0BGround. Every page starts here. Warm, never blue-black.
Notation Ivory · #EDE6D8All type. The colour of the paper his notes are printed on.
Honorific Brass · #C4933EEarned things only — titles, awards, years, section marks.
Temple Indigo · #1B2140Lesson interface surfaces. Separates study from story.
Kumkum · #C6402FOne use per page, maximum. The enrol action. Nothing else.

Type — four voices

Instrument Serif DISPLAY · headlines, pull quotes, numbers. High contrast, cinematic, not a wedding font.
Archivo — interface and body UI · paragraphs, labels, forms. Neutral, wide, legible at 15px.
கைத்தல நிறைகனி — திருப்புகழ் TAMIL · a first-class typeface, set at its own optical size. Never a fallback.
RAGA MOHANAM · THALA AADHI · D#-2.5 TECHNICAL · raga, thala, sruthi, speed, swara. Data, treated as data.
Geometry

Hairline rules at 1px, spaced like staff lines. Square corners everywhere — no rounded cards, no pills. A wide left margin on every page that echoes the margin of a notation sheet. Full-bleed photography, never inset in a frame.

Material

Fine paper grain over ink. Brass with a real specular edge for awards. Deep vignettes so the subject emerges from darkness rather than sitting on a colour. No glass, no frosted panels, no decorative gradients.

Photography

Single key light from stage left, hard falloff, warm. Subject held to one third of the frame so copy owns the other two. Hands and bow shown as often as face — this is a craft, and the craft is in the hands.

Explicit exclusions

No stock imagery of unrelated violinists. No temple clip-art. No Om symbols as decoration. No rainbow gradients despite the domain name. No emoji — the current site uses 👉 as a download affordance; it goes.

06

The motion system — one held note

Motion personality: slow bow. Long eases of 700–1200ms, entering along the direction of a bow stroke. Nothing bounces. The only fast element in the whole site is the swara ticker. The hero is a single pinned sequence with scroll mapped to normalised progress from 0 to 1, and every layer answers to that one clock.

ProgressBeatWhat the visitor seesWhat the interface does
0.00 Arrival Black. A single sustained note. The portrait resolves out of darkness, lit from stage left. Video frame 0 = poster. Name and lineage set in ivory, left third.
0.20 Reveal A line of Tamil swara notation writes itself across the frame, in his own notation, at bow speed. SVG stroke-dashoffset drawn on scrub. Copy 1 out, copy 2 in.
0.50 Transform The Tamil line transposes into English, glyph by glyph. This is the invention, shown rather than claimed. Theme shifts ink → indigo. The single most important 200ms on the site.
0.78 Proof Twelve honorific titles stack in brass, each with its awarding body and year. Then the record: 5,000 stages, AIR since 1992. Cards in on stagger. Nav switches to its condensed state.
1.00 Resolution The note releases and hands you the library — 22 lessons, free, in two languages. Unpin resolves directly into the lesson grid. Enrol CTA arrives last.

Mobile takes a different branch, not a squeeze: no pin, no scrubbed video. A poster frame, the notation-to-English transposition as a tap-triggered two-state animation, and the honorifics as a horizontal scroller. Reduced-motion serves the poster and the final state with no scrub at all.

The one thing people will remember

Every lesson in his archive already ships practice audio in five sruthis across three speeds, with violin and vocal recorded separately. On the current site that is a list of thirty identical Google Drive links. It is the most valuable thing he has made and the worst-presented. It becomes an instrument.

The lesson page, as a product
01

Notation, in either language, on the page. Not a Drive link that opens a new tab. Rendered, zoomable, printable, with a Tamil/English switch that keeps your scroll position.

02

The three-stage progression, named. Step by step → practise together → karaoke. Amma Aanandadayni already has all five parts. Make that the template for every lesson.

03

Metadata as navigation. Raga, thala, composer, level. Twenty-two lessons become browsable by raga — Mohanam, Nattai, Malahari, Mayamalavagowlai — which is how musicians actually think.

04

One honest ask at the bottom. "You have the notes. Now get the correction." A trial lesson, priced, bookable, in the student's timezone.

How we build it

Front of the pipeline to the back, following the playbook you sent. Reference intelligence and art direction before a single asset. Assets approved before code. Motion mapped before it is animated. Nothing invented at implementation time that was already decided.

STAGE 1 · done

Content extraction

Both sites, all pages, 22 lessons, 68 media assets, every defect logged. Sitting in data/.

STAGE 2 · this document

Art direction locked

Thesis, palette with roles, four type voices, geometry, material, motion personality, exclusions.

STAGE 3 · next

Hero concept

Build the first view before anything else — it sets the standard the rest of the site is measured against.

STAGE 4

Asset production

Portrait and hands at directed crops, desktop and mobile. Brass award plates. Poster frame matched to video frame 0.

STAGE 5

Motion asset

Six seconds, camera locked, subject held to the right third for the whole clip so left-aligned copy stays clear. No cuts.

STAGE 6

Implementation

Smallest stack that reproduces the choreography exactly. Scroll-linked timeline for the hero, CSS for everything else.

STAGE 7

Responsive as art direction

Desktop, tablet, mobile composed independently. Both sides of every breakpoint tested. Focal exclusion zone respected at each.

STAGE 8

Browser QA

Load and poster state, hero motion to final handoff, breakpoint edges, keyboard and focus, video seeking, clean console.

What blocks the build

Blocking
  • A pricing decision. Per lesson, per month, or per term — and the number. Nothing on the enrol page can be designed without it.
  • Photography. One directed session: portrait at stage-left key light, hands and bow, and a six-second locked-camera clip. Everything currently on the site is web-sized and cannot carry a full-bleed hero.
  • The logo as vector. Two versions exist as raster PNG. A hero at this scale needs the original.
Needed before launch
  • Titles for the six untitled 2023 posts, and real copy for the two headlines still reading Loreip.
  • One home for the notation PDFs — currently split between Google Drive and Dropbox, which will break the in-page reader.
  • Which claim is true: 37 years, 30+ years, or 25. The three appear on the same two sites today. I will use 37 unless told otherwise.
  • Names of the universities using his notation system, or we drop that claim rather than assert it unsourced.
Already handled
  • All copy from both sites, verbatim.
  • All 22 lessons with notation, audio, video and metadata.
  • 68 media assets inventoried and hotlinkable.
  • Testimonials, comments, honours, education, radio and television record.
