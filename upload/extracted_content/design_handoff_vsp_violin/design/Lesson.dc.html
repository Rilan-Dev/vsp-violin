How to work through it

Three stages, in order.

The same progression Suka Pavalan uses in class. Do not move to the next speed until the current one is clean at every exercise.

1
Step by step Recite the swaram aloud before you play it. Watch the video, pause after each line, match the fingering.
2
Practise together Play along with the track at your sruthi. Stay at first speed until every exercise is even.
3
On your own Drop the track and play unaccompanied. If it falls apart without the recording, go back to stage two.
