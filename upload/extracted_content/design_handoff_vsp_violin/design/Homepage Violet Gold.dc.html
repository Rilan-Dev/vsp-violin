The guru

Six years old when he started. Eight when he first played to a room.

Suka Pavalan holds an M.A. in Thamizhisai and a Bachelor of Music in violin from Annamalai University, alongside a teacher-training diploma in music. He has organised, coordinated and performed in live and televised concerts nationally and internationally, and composes in both Carnatic and Western traditions.

The achievement he names first is not an award. It is the method he created for converting Carnatic notation into multiple languages, now used across universities and colleges.
