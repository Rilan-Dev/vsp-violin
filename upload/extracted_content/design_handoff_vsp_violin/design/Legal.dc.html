Three things to correct before launch
Governing law says Netherlands

The Terms cite “prevailing law of Netherlands” — a leftover from the generator template. The business operates from Puducherry, India.

Comment clauses no longer apply

Several paragraphs govern user comments. The new site has no comments — enquiries go through a private form. Either cut those clauses or keep them for the enquiry form.

Placeholder domain and account language

The Privacy Policy references “www.website.com” and describes registering an Account. There are no visitor accounts on this site.
